FULL DESCRIPTION (<=4000):
Pop, spin, click and fidget. Fidget is a calm antistress set of four sensory toys with real physics, rich sound and phone haptics. No ads, ever, and it works offline.

4 TOYS, ALL FREE
BUBBLE WRAP: Bubbles that pop with a crisp snap and spring back, so you never run out.
SPIN: A weighty fidget spinner with true momentum. Flick it and watch the RPM climb.
CLICK: Nine keys, nine different feels. Clicky, tactile and silent switches, a push-latch with an ON light, a charge key you hold and release, a double-tap key, a rapid-fire key, a rocker and a typewriter key with a bell.
CUBE: A fidget cube face. Soft musical buttons, a flip toggle, a dial with clicks, a spring-back joystick and a roller.

CALM, CRAVINGS & FOCUS
• "Ride it out": a 3-minute timer with a breathing pacer to sit through a craving or an urge
• Calm mode: muted colours, softer sound and gentler vibration
• Something for restless hands while you study, wait or focus

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
• Smooth 60 fps physics, sound tied to how you touch, vibration on supported phones
• Personal records with a celebration when you beat one

RESPECTFUL BY DESIGN
• Zero ads. No accounts, no loot boxes, nothing pay-to-win
• Works offline after first launch. Settings and stats stay on your device
• Optional one-time extras: Fidget Premium adds glow, glitter, neon and confetti looks to every toy, and each toy has its own upgrade looks with their own sounds and effects. Try any Premium look free for a few seconds first.

Fidget is a relaxation toy for fun. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition.
