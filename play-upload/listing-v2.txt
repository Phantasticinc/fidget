APP NAME (<=30):
Fidget: Pop It & Antistress

SHORT DESCRIPTION (<=80):
Pop it, slime, squishy & fidget cube. Antistress toys with no ads, offline.

FULL DESCRIPTION (<=4000):
Pop it, squeeze, stretch and click. Fidget is a calm antistress set of sensory toys with real physics, rich sound and phone haptics. No ads, ever, and it works offline.

9 TOYS, ALL FREE
POP IT: A rainbow silicone push-pop. Every dome snaps through with a rubbery thup. Press them all and the board flips over for a fresh side.
SLIME: Glossy, stretchy slime. Pull the edge and it stretches and peels off your finger. Poke it, or pop the little air bubbles inside.
SQUISH: A memory-foam stress ball. Press it and it dimples and bulges, then rises back slowly. Pinch the edge to squeeze.
CUBE: A fidget cube face. Clicky and silent buttons, a flip toggle, a dial with clicks, a spring-back joystick and a roller.
SWITCH: A wall of chunky light switches. Each crisp flick lights a lamp. Swipe across to flick a row.
BUBBLE WRAP: Bubbles that pop with a crisp snap and spring back, so you never run out.
SPIN: A weighty spinner with true momentum.
CLICK: Keys and switches tuned to feel like the real thing.
SAND: Kinetic sand you can slice, press and pinch.

CALM, CRAVINGS & FOCUS
• "Ride it out": a 3-minute timer with a breathing pacer to sit through a craving or an urge
• Calm mode: muted colours, softer sound and gentler vibration
• Something for restless hands while you study, wait or focus

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
• Smooth 60 fps physics, sound tied to how you touch, vibration on supported phones
• Personal records with a celebration when you beat one

RESPECTFUL BY DESIGN
• Zero ads. No accounts, no loot boxes, nothing pay-to-win
• Works offline after first launch. Settings and stats stay on your device
• Optional one-time extras: Fidget Premium adds glow, glitter, neon and confetti looks to every toy, and per-toy upgrades are available too. Try any Premium look free for a few seconds first.

Fidget is a relaxation toy for fun. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition.
