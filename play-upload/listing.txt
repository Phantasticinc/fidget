APP NAME (<=30):
Fidget: Tactile Toys

SHORT DESCRIPTION (<=80):
Pop, spin, click & slice kinetic sand. Real haptics, no ads, works offline.

FULL DESCRIPTION (<=4000):
Fidget is a pocket set of tactile toys built with real-time physics, rich sound and phone haptics. Something satisfying for your hands, any time.

POP — A grid of bubbles that pop with a crisp snap and spring back, so you never run out.
SPIN — A weighty spinner with true momentum. Flick it hard and watch it coast.
CLICK — Crisp switches and buttons tuned to feel like the real thing.
SAND — Kinetic sand you can slice, press and pinch. Cut walls crumble, grains trickle and settle, and Mold presses a fresh shape in a new color.

Made to feel good:
• Smooth 60 fps physics
• Sound tied to how you touch, plus vibration on supported phones
• Calm mode for softer, quieter play
• "Ride it out" timer for a short focus break

Respectful by design:
• No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases
• No data collected — your settings stay on your device
• Works offline after first launch

Fidget is a relaxation toy for fun. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition.
