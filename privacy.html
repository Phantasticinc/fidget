Privacy Policy

Updated October 25, 2026 · Phantastic Vanguard

Fidget has no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs. Effective October 25, 2026 (v1.1).

On your device: settings (sound, haptics, calm mode, volume, theme), equipped upgrades, a cached list of your purchases, your nickname and your toy stats are saved only in local app storage. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes them.

Purchases: Premium and upgrades are bought through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we never see your card or Google account details. The app only receives which products you own, to unlock them.

Leaderboard (optional, online only if enabled): to rank scores, the app sends your chosen nickname, your toy scores, the week, and a random device ID generated by the app to our leaderboard server (hosted by Supabase). No real names, email, location or contacts are collected. There is no chat. Nicknames are filtered for offensive words. To delete your leaderboard entries, email us your nickname and we will remove them within 30 days; entries are otherwise kept while the leaderboard runs.

The app uses your device's vibration motor and speaker for feedback. It requests no permissions beyond vibration and does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Not a medical device. Fidget is a relaxation and focus toy. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition, including nicotine dependence, anxiety, or autism. For quitting tobacco, visit smokefree.gov or call 1-800-QUIT-NOW (US).

Intended for users 13 and older. Contact: info@phantasticvanguard.com
